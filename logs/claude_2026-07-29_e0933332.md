## OpenAI Signals — closer, but the topic taxonomy kills it

This is the more interesting near-miss. Signals is a real, ongoing, **CC BY 4.0** data release with downloadable CSVs, monthly, July 2024–March 2026, 300k sampled messages/month. It even has US state data. But:

**The fatal problem is that "topic" doesn't mean subject matter.** OpenAI's taxonomy is *functional* — 24 capability categories: `computer_programming`, `cooking_and_recipes`, `edit_or_critique_provided_text`, `tutoring_or_teaching`, `create_an_image`, and so on. Two further blockers even if a politics topic existed:

- **State × topic is annual, not monthly.** The only such file is `usa_share_of_messages_by_topic_state_2025.csv`, documented as `P(topic | year, state_code)` — 2025 only. Every *monthly* topic series is global or country-level, and the country × topic × month series "is not shared in CSV format." So state × month × topic simply doesn't exist.

Google has subject-matter-ish categories but only country × one fortnight; OpenAI has state and month but a functional taxonomy that has no concept of politics.

That's worth saying plainly in the memo — it's a genuine contribution claim, not just a data-access convenience.
