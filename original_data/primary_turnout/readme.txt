primary_turnout_2026.csv
Hand-compiled 2026 primary ballots cast and adult population for the eight
May-primary states in the analysis sample. Compiled 7/31/2026.

ballots - total ballots cast in the state's May 2026 primary:
- AL: 879,785, official SOS total via Alabama Reflector (5/28/2026).
- GA: ~1,976,000 = Dem US Senate primary 1,041,976 (Ballotpedia) + Rep
  governor primary ~934,000 (press reports); top contest per party. Georgia
  does not publish a single certified ballots-cast total in accessible
  sources; widely reported "850,000+" figures were early voting only.
- ID: 310,030 = certified 30.1% turnout x ~1,030,000 registered
  (themidtermproject.org tracker; registration from state reports).
- IN: 807,059, official. Indiana SOS "2026 Primary Registration and Turnout
  Data" spreadsheet (statewide row: 4,713,746 registered, 17% turnout),
  saved in this folder as in_2026_primary_turnout.xlsx.
- LA: ~832,000, SOS total via press coverage of the May 16 election.
- OH: 1,791,152, certified official canvass (Secretary LaRose certification,
  6/3/2026, "2026 Ohio Primary Election By the Numbers"; 23% of registered).
- OR: 1,300,458 = certified 41.9% x 3,103,717 registered
  (themidtermproject.org tracker).
- PA: ~1,740,000 = Dem governor >1.1M (Shapiro) + Rep governor ~640,000
  (Garrity), Spotlight PA; top contest per party. Consistent with the
  ">1.7 million ballots" reported by WITF. The certified statewide total is
  on electionreturns.pa.gov but not accessible to automated collection.

vap_2024 - civilian population age 18+, July 2024 estimates, US Census
Bureau SC-EST2024-AGESEX-CIV (sc-est2024-agesex-civ.csv, sum of single-year
ages 18+ with SEX=0), downloaded 7/31/2026 from
https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/popest/datasets/2020-2024/state/asrh/
