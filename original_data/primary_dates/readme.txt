primary_dates_2026.csv
Hand-compiled 2026 statewide primary election dates (federal/state offices).

Sources (accessed 7/27/2026):
- NCSL, "2026 State Primary Election Dates"
  https://www.ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns/2026-state-primary-election-dates
- FVAP, "2026 Primary Elections by state and territory" (PDF saved in this folder
  as fvap_primary_elections_calendar_2026.pdf; "current as of May 2026")
  https://www.fvap.gov/uploads/FVAP/VAO/PrimaryElectionsCalendar.pdf

The two sources agree on every state. DC comes from FVAP only (June 16).
Notes column flags special cases: Louisiana's May 16 primary covers the US Senate
seat and local offices only (US House uses the Nov 3 jungle primary); Alabama holds
primaries for certain redistricted US House seats on Aug 11 per NCSL (main date
May 19 used here); Wisconsin held its nonpartisan spring election (state supreme
court) on Apr 7 2026, separate from its Aug 11 partisan primary.
